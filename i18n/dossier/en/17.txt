2.1 Petrol, epsilon_SR=-0.25,epsilon_LR=-0.60.
a) Price +5% ->quantity about -1.25% in the short run, -3% in the long run.
   Possible adjustments: trips, public transport, vehicle, place of residence.

   method taught. If "constant elasticity at every price" is
   read as Q=A*p^-0.6, exact calculation p1/p0=0.85^(-1/0.6),
   i.e. about +31.11%. State the convention; 25% is the exercise's usual
   linear approximation, not the exact solution for this function.

2.2 I1200, p_L40,p_R120,U=L*R.
Budget 40L+120R<=1200; L=30-3R with R horizontal, L vertical.
Intercepts: L30,R10.

(24,2): spending 1200,U48, affordable.
