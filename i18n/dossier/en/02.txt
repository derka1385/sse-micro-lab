0. HOW TO USE THIS FILE AND WHAT IT COVERS
======================================================================

Goal: understand the mechanisms, recognise which model to apply,
know how to rebuild the formulas, calculate, draw and explain in English.
This dossier is a teaching summary, not an official SSE answer key.
Numerical results presented as "recalculated" were derived from the
exam/assignment texts. Personal answers in the assignments are not treated
as an authority. The main errors or ambiguities are flagged.

Full corpus of the Microeconomics folder: 23 PDFs, 373 pages.
12 lectures, 5 assignments numbered 2 to 6, 6 final exams 2020 to 2025.
Text extracted from every page; visual review of the lectures and
assignments, checking tables embedded as images and the
relevant calculations. The last page of the 2020 exam is blank.
Page counts include covers, instructions and blank pages.

Limits of the corpus, to keep in any sheet produced from it:
- No assignment 1 or 7 is present.
- Only lectures 1 to 12 are present. The Lecture 1 schedule
  still announces platforms and international trade in week 7.
- Auctions and some game-theory elements appear in the
  exams or assignments without a matching full lecture here.
- The six past-exam PDFs are FINAL exams, not midterms,
  and contain no official solutions.
- A frequency over six past exams describes this sample; it is
  not a probability of appearing in the next exam.
- Dates, rules and current-affairs examples are those of the materials.
  This dossier does not check the current administrative rules on Canvas.

Provenance markers: L1 to L12 = lectures; A2 to A6 = assignments;
E2020 to E2025 = exams. "p." means the PDF page, counted from 1.
"Complement" = a standard explanation rebuilt to handle an exercise,
not a claim that an extra lecture was found.

PROMPT TO GIVE CHATGPT WITH THIS FILE
"You are my microeconomics tutor for the SSE BE 501 exam. Use this
dossier as the main context. Explain in English, keeping the English
terms and abbreviations I will meet in the exam. For each
new symbol, spell out its English name, explain it, give its units
and its role. For each formula, explain where it comes from, why it
holds, its assumptions, how to recognise when to use it and when it
does not apply. First make me identify the objective, the constraints,
the choice variable and the model. Suggest progressive exercises,
let me try, then correct my reasoning and my graphs.
Also train me to give short, justified answers in English.
Distinguish documented elements, recalculated results and
complements. Do not invent an official answer key, a frequency or missing
syllabus content. If I ask for sheets, a website or exercises, also cover
the edge cases and pitfalls listed here."
