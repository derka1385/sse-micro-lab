======================================================================
18. DIRECTORY OF ABBREVIATIONS AND SYMBOLS
======================================================================

Marking: [O] abbreviation/symbol observed in the PDFs; [A] standard alias
of a notion explicitly present but not necessarily written as such;
[C] standard complement added to make the methods understandable.
Variants are grouped. This directory covers the corpus and its useful
variants, not every abbreviation in every microeconomics textbook.

18.1 Costs, revenue, production and profit
AC [O] — Average Cost — average cost; generally equivalent to ATC here,
  C(Q)/Q. Check whether an author uses it for another average cost.
AFC [O] — Average Fixed Cost — average fixed cost; FC/Q.
AP, APL, AP_L [A] — Average Product (of Labor) — average product (of labour); Q/L.
ATC [O] — Average Total Cost — average total cost; TC/Q.
AVC [O] — Average Variable Cost — average variable cost; VC/Q.
C(Q), TC [O] — (Total) Cost — total cost function, FC+VC(Q).
FC [O] — Fixed Cost — fixed cost; independent of Q over the horizon studied.
F [O] — Fixed cost OU Fixed fee — fixed production cost OR fee
  collected. Identify the role; the two have opposite signs in pi.
MC [O] — Marginal Cost — marginal cost; dC/dQ, money/unit of output.
MP [O] — Marginal Product — marginal product of a factor, output/input.
MPL, MP_L [O] — Marginal Product of Labor — marginal product of labour.
MPK, MP_K [A] — Marginal Product of Capital — marginal product of capital.
MR [O] — Marginal Revenue — marginal revenue; dR/dQ.
MRP_L [C] — Marginal Revenue Product of Labor — marginal revenue brought
  by an extra unit of labour input, MR*MP_L.
VMP_L [A] — Value of Marginal Product of Labor — value of the marginal
  product of labour, p*MP_L; competitive hiring condition.
CSR [A] — Corporate Social Responsibility — the firm's social
  responsibility, raised in the discussion of the profit objective in L6.
R(Q), Revenue [O] ; TR [A] — Total Revenue — total revenue, p(Q)*Q.
AR [C] — Average Revenue — average revenue, R/Q; equal to the price under a single price.
VC [O] — Variable Cost — variable cost, depends on Q.
pi, Π, π [O] — Profit — profit/economic profit depending on the costs included.
A, TFP [A] — Total Factor Productivity — total factor productivity,
  efficiency in Q=A*f(K,L). A is not always a technology parameter.
K [O] — Capital — productive capital; does not automatically mean money.
L [O] — Labor — labour; BUT L can also be the Low type or Lunches.
J [O] — Land dans L5 — land; letter chosen for this example.
w [O] — Wage — wage/cost per unit of labour.
r [O] — Rental rate of capital — rental price of one unit of capital.
q_i [O] — Firm i's quantity — quantity produced by firm i.
Q [O] — Total quantity/output — market quantity or output; read the context.
Qbar, Q avec barre [O] — imposed target output, not a freely chosen variable.
HP [O] — Highly Productive — highly productive firm, L8.
LP [O] — Less Productive — less productive firm, L8.
SR, LR [A] — Short Run, Long Run — short run/long run.
SRAC, LRAC [A] — Short-/Long-Run Average Cost — average cost by horizon.
RTS [A] — Returns To Scale — returns to scale.
IRS, CRS, DRS [A] — Increasing/Constant/Decreasing Returns to Scale —
  increasing/constant/decreasing returns; distinguish from cost economies.

18.2 Demand, consumer and elasticities
p, P [O] — Price — price per unit. Upper/lower case has no universal
  meaning: the same material may vary its notation.
QD, Q^D, Q_D [O] — Quantity Demanded — quantity demanded at a price.
QS, Q^S, Q_S [O] — Quantity Supplied — quantity supplied at a price.
D, S [O] — Demand, Supply — demand/supply curves on graphs.
  D and S can also denote a loan, a player or a group elsewhere.
p_b, pBuyer [O] — Buyer price — price paid by the buyer.
p_s, pSeller [O] — Seller price — net price received by the seller.
U(x,y) [O] — Utility — utility, an ordinal representation of preferences.
MU, MU_x [O] — Marginal Utility — marginal utility of good x.
MRS [O] — Marginal Rate of Substitution — marginal rate of substitution,
  ratio of marginal utilities and absolute value of the indifference slope.
MRT [O] — Marginal Rate of Transformation — marginal rate of transformation,
  absolute slope of the PPF in the production model; in a consumption budget,
  the relative price is sometimes called the available rate of transformation/exchange.
MRTS [A] — Marginal Rate of Technical Substitution — marginal rate of
  technical substitution; ratio of MPs, absolute slope of the isoquant.
I [O] — Income — consumer's income/budget; sometimes investment elsewhere.
E [O] — Entertainment dans L3 — entertainment; not always elasticity.
F [O] — Food dans L3 — food; elsewhere F=fixed cost/access fee.
G, O [O] — Gasoline, Other goods — petrol/other goods in L4.
PL, PR, p_L, p_R [O] — lunch/record prices in A2; elsewhere the subscripts
  L/R can denote the low type or Red. Always read the local definition.
ED, E^D, E_D, epsilon_D, epsilon_d [O/A] — (Own-)Price Elasticity of Demand
  — price elasticity of demand, usually negatively signed.
epsilon_S, ES [A] — Price Elasticity of Supply — price elasticity of supply.
epsilon_ij, E_cross [A] — Cross-Price Elasticity — cross-price elasticity.
eta, epsilon_I, E_I [A] — Income Elasticity — income elasticity.
WTP [A] — Willingness To Pay — willingness to pay; per unit if marginal,
  sum/area if total. A total WTP is not automatically the marginal price.
Delta I [O] — compensation/change in income; lump-sum transfer in L4.
p_r [O] — price of another good, e.g. raspberries; not the r of capital.
T [O] — temperature/demand determinant in L2; elsewhere transfer/tax.

18.3 Welfare, policies and externalities
CS, C.S. [O] — Consumer Surplus — consumer surplus.
PS [O] — Producer Surplus — producer surplus; revenue minus VC.
W, TS [A] — Welfare, Total Surplus — welfare/total monetary surplus.
DWL [O] — Deadweight Loss — deadweight loss, net surplus lost.
MB [O/A] — Marginal Benefit — marginal benefit; WTP for the next unit.
MPC, PMC, MC_p [A/O] — Marginal Private Cost — marginal private cost.
MEC, MC_e [O] — Marginal External Cost — marginal external cost.
MSC, SMC [A] — Marginal Social Cost — marginal social cost, MPC+MEC.
MPB, PMB [A] — Marginal Private Benefit — marginal private benefit.
MEB [O] — Marginal External Benefit — marginal external benefit.
MSB [O], SMB [A] — Marginal Social Benefit — marginal social benefit, MPB+MEB.
MAC [O] — Marginal Abatement Cost — marginal cost of abating/reducing
  emissions, not "marginal average cost".
EC, EB [A] — External Cost, External Benefit — TOTAL external cost/benefit,
  when so defined; distinguish from marginal MEC/MEB.
CPR [O] — Common-Pool Resource / Common Property Resource — common resource,
  Q_CPR in the L11 open-access graph.
t, tau [O/A] — Unit Tax / Tariff — unit tax or customs duty.
s [O] — Unit Subsidy — unit subsidy; elsewhere a probability or a state.
T, Tax revenue [A/O] — tax revenue, t*Q; a monetary amount.
Qpriv, Qopt, Qeff, Q* [O/A] — private, optimal, efficient and
  equilibrium quantities. A star alone does NOT say "social optimum".
QPoll [O] — Quantity of Pollution — quantity of pollution in L11.
VAT [O] — Value Added Tax — value added tax; an ad valorem tax in general, simplified
  to a unit tax in the E2025 exercise.

18.4 Markets, contracts and games
Lerner, L_index [A] — Lerner Index — (p-MC)/p; avoid L if L=labour.
Pm, p_M, QM, Q_M [O] — monopoly price/quantity on graphs.
PC, p_C [O] — in L10, the COURNOT price; elsewhere PC can mean
  Perfect Competition. The subscript must be read within its figure.
PPT, p_PT [O] — price-taking price on the L10 p.10 figure.
QC, Q_C [O] — competitive quantity in monopoly graphs;
  in E2025 Qc means the quantity of CONCERT tickets, not competition.
MC_i, c_i [O] — marginal cost of firm i, constant when written c_i.
n [O] — number of firms, cafés or agents depending on context.
BR, BR_i [A] — Best Response — best response/reaction function.
NE [A] — Nash Equilibrium — Nash equilibrium.
SPNE [A] — Subgame Perfect Nash Equilibrium — subgame perfect equilibrium.
IC [O] — Incentive Compatibility — incentive compatibility; the agent
  chooses the contract/action the designer wants to induce.
IR [O] — Individual Rationality — participation; the contract is at least as
  attractive as the outside option.
H, L [O] — High/Low — types, quality or effort; do not assume the same meaning everywhere.
B [O] — private benefit of low effort in E2023/A6; in L9 B_H
  denotes a bundle, and in L10 B=Blue.
R [O] — revenue in credit; R=Red in oligopoly; R=Records in A2.
F, T_j [O/A] — fixed fee, payment for menu j; their role depends on the contract.
S, N [O] — Split/Steal in A3, choices in this assignment; SN/NS/NN are
  strategy profiles, not new economic notions.
E[X] [A] — Expected Value — expectation of X, weighted average over states.
pH, pL [A] — probabilities of success under action H/L in the contract;
  elsewhere p_H,p_L are PRICES for the high/low groups.
d [A] — probability of detection in the inspection example; another
  exercise may use d as a supply slope.

18.5 Trade and mathematics
PPF, PPC [A] — Production Possibility Frontier/Curve — production
  possibility frontier, given by technology and resources.
OC [A] — Opportunity Cost — opportunity cost, the best alternative forgone.
M [A] — Imports — imports, QD-QS in an importing country.
pw, p_W [A] — World Price — world price, given in the small-country model.
ToT [A] — Terms of Trade — terms of trade, export prices/import prices.
FOC [C] — First-Order Condition — first-order condition, zero derivative
  at an interior optimum; standard term for the conditions used in the course.
SOC [C] — Second-Order Condition — second-order condition, checks the curvature.
Delta, Δ [O] — finite change, final value minus initial value.
d/dx [O] — derivative with respect to a variable.
partial, ∂ [O] — partial derivative, other variables held constant.
prime, ' [O] — another notation for a derivative, e.g. C'(Q).
max/min [O] — maximise/minimise an objective.
s.t. [C] — Subject To — subject to the constraint.
sqrt(x), racine(x) [O/A] — square root; x^(1/2).
barre [O] — fixed/target variable; star [O] — optimisation/equilibrium solution.

18.6 Contextual non-conceptual abbreviations
SSE=Stockholm School of Economics; BE501=course code; ID=student
identifier; AI=Artificial Intelligence; GPT=name of a family of AI models;
US=United States; UK=United Kingdom; EU=European Union; kr=kronor;
USD=US dollars; GB/LN are names of actors/places in exercises;
HIV=virus cited as a medicine example; MTB=mountain bike;
CSCE=Conference on Security and Co-operation in Europe in A3;
SFA-Oxford=source of a graph; I/II/III may be part numbers.
These acronyms are not microeconomics formulas.
